# Historical Bust Rates by Round

## Definition
A "bust" = player finishes 30+ fantasy points below expected value at draft position (PPR).

## Approximate bust rates by round (redraft)

| Round | RB bust % | WR bust % | QB bust % | TE bust % |
|-------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| 1 | 15-20% | 12-18% | 25-35% | 20-25% |
| 2 | 20-25% | 18-22% | 20-30% | 25-30% |
| 3 | 25-30% | 22-28% | 15-25% | 30-35% |
| 4-6 | 30-40% | 25-35% | 10-20% | 35-45% |
| 7+ | 40-50% | 35-45% | 15-25% | 40-50% |

## Implications
- Round 1 busts are devastating — prioritize proven production over upside.
- Mid-round RBs (rounds 3-6) have highest bust rates — this is the "RB dead zone" in some eras.
- Late-round busts are cheap — acceptable cost for upside plays.
- QB/TE early picks carry higher bust risk relative to replacement value.

## Risk management
- Diversify risk: don't stack multiple "upside" picks in rounds 1-4.
- In rounds 4-6, favor WR over RB if ADP is equal (PPR historical edge).
- Track your personal bust rate by round — if round 3-4 bust rate > 35%, tighten criteria.
