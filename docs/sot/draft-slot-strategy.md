# Draft Slot Strategy (Snake)

## Pick 1-3 (Early)
- **Strength:** First crack at elite talent; back-to-back in round 2.
- **Strategy:** Take best RB/WR available. Use the turn to grab two starters (e.g., RB + WR or WR + WR in PPR).
- **Avoid:** Reaching for QB/TE at the turn because of long wait — only if elite value.

## Pick 4-7 (Middle)
- **Strength:** Flexibility — can react to the board. Often get a tier drop at the turn.
- **Strategy:** Don't lock into a plan. Let the board fall, then attack value at the turn.
- **Avoid:** Predicting who will be there — have 3-4 targets for each pick.

## Pick 8-10 (Late)
- **Strength:** Three picks in rounds 1-3 (if 10-team: picks 8, 13, 20 or similar depending on slot).
- **Strategy:** Anchor with two elite players at the turn, then best available in round 3.
- **Avoid:** Reaching for "third RB" when WR value is better in PPR.

## Snake math reminder
- Round 2 pick 10 in a 10-team = overall pick 11.
- Track **overall pick number**, not just round — ADP is in overall picks.

## Turn planning
Before draft, list:
1. Your ideal pick at current slot.
2. Fallback if tier break happens.
3. Position you'd take at the turn if run occurs.
