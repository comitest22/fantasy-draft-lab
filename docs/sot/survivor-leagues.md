# Survivor leagues

Strategy is a hub (`/strategy`) with four cards, same layout as Tools: **Draft**, **NFL Survivor**, **NFL Survivor Loser**, **TD Streak**. Draft is the old Strategy page (`/strategy/draft`).

## NFL Survivor (winner)

Route `/strategy/survivor`. Source of truth is the PoolGenius Data Grid: [poolgenius.teamrankings.com/nfl-survivor-pool-picks/data-grid](https://poolgenius.teamrankings.com/nfl-survivor-pool-picks/data-grid/).

Snapshot file: `data/enrichment/poolgenius-survivor-2026.json`. Refresh with `npm run enrichment:fetch-poolgenius` (uses `POOLGENIUS_USERNAME` / `POOLGENIUS_PASSWORD` from `.env`; week from `--week` or `season-state.json`). The fetch **logs out, signs up as the next numbered address (`name26` → `name27`, saved in `poolgenius-account.json`), then logs in** before reading the grid. A retry within 18 hours reuses that account. If the page says the week is not ready until after 5PM Eastern, the previous snapshot stays. Do not commit credentials. Tuesday/Friday job: `docs/sot/weekly-refresh.md`.

Grid fields: team, opponent, PoolGenius win %, market win %, moneyline, spread, pick popularity, week EV, future value, game notes (Away / Div / Thu / Wed / SNF / MNF), plus projected win % vs opponent for weeks 1–18. After a week is final, **Season** cells keep that last projected % (they are not rewritten to 100 / 0). The Tuesday/Friday job (`fetch-poolgenius` after `fetch-sos-schedule`) **aligns** season columns to `nfl-schedule-2026.csv` by matching opponents (PoolGenius often shifts remaining weeks by one after finals), then **locks** finished team-weeks with **W / L + score** from the schedule; remaining weeks keep the remapped PoolGenius %. The board also overlays schedule opp/W/L at serve time. Picks survival still treats a finished W as 100% and L as 0% so a locked ticket stays on the board. If the JSON is already wrong without a new PoolGenius pull, `npx tsx scripts/enrichment/align-poolgenius-schedule.ts` rewrites opps/% from the schedule match.

**Week** view is that week’s table, sorted by recommended pick rank. Rank uses PoolGenius EV (when that week’s board was saved) plus win % minus a small popularity and future-value tax so chalk + high-FV sides drop a bit, then a **capped ±4** rest/travel/weather/division overlay (`server/src/analysis/gameContext.ts`). Top 3 are highlighted. Notes column shows PoolGenius flags plus context labels (home after road streak, short week, divisional, timezone travel, poor road record, outdoor wind/precip/cold). Click a team to mark it used (`localStorage` `dsafd-survivor-win-v1`). Each fetch stores that week’s Mkt / ML / Spread / Pop / EV / notes on `weekBoards[week]` so past weeks keep the full board after the slate advances; weeks never pulled while current still show projected win odds only.

**Season** view is the 18-week win-% grid (PoolGenius season view). Finished cells show the **locked projection**, then **W/L and the score** (`W 41-21`); they are not recast as 100 or 0. Upcoming and finished week boxes are the **same size**, with **8px** gap between cells so the grid is not flush. The selected week is marked on the **week number** in the header (not a ring around every cell in that column). Click a week number to sort teams by that week’s displayed %: first click descending (highest % on top), second click ascending (BYE / missing % last). Week chips and Season cells use the same **green → white** heat as Tools → SOS (`pctHeat` in `client/src/utils/heatScale.ts`): high displayed % is accent green, low % is palette white, with the same `√t` ease so mid favorites are not all mint. Finished cells keep that heat from the stored projection and add a **3px green/red border** for W/L (no solid white/green fill). If week 1 ever lost its locked PoolGenius %, the board seeds it from a static Week 1 2026 favorite map (`pgWeek1Seed.ts`) so Season still shows rating + heat. The percent scale is stretched so ~80%+ is full green and ~40%- is full white (typical NFL odds, steeper than a 20% floor). NFL Survivor Loser colors the lose % the same way (high lose % = green).

**Picks** view is for one or more tickets. Each row has a dropdown on the first unlocked week (starts at week 1). The closed dropdown is the same heat square as leftover weeks (team, vs/@, %). Clicking it opens a custom list **to the right** of the square (portaled so the table scroll does not clip it; flips left if there is not enough room). The first unlocked (clickable) square uses a **3px white ring** at rest, same thickness as played W/L cells. Focus or an open list **keeps the heat fill** and that same white ring (no solid green overlay). Locked weeks with no W/L yet drop heat: **pale `#E2E8F0` fill and 3px pale ring** (same family as played cells, without green/red). Leftover unlocked weeks stay heat-only with no ring. The **current NFL week** (`board.currentWeek` from `season-state.json`) has a two-line **Current Week** label and a **green week number** on one shared header background (not a pill). Lock that pick to freeze it and move the dropdown to the next week, pre-filled with the suggested leftover pick for that week; a closed lock under a frozen box unlocks back to that week, and later weeks keep their last selected team. Hovering a lock shows the opposite icon (unlocked → locked, locked → unlocked) with a green circle. Unlocked hover uses the thicker green ring; locked hover and any press keep that same ring. Locked circles keep the same fill as unlocked (`--surface`). Press also nudges the button down. Lock and leftover padlocks sit on a small circle button at all times, with space under each square, between neighboring week cells, below each lock (so the next row’s boxes are not flush), and after week 18 so the last column is not flush with the table border. Played cells drop the green heat: same pale fill as a loss, **3px green border** on W and **3px red border** on L. Remaining unlocked weeks are filled jointly, one cell per ticket per round: tickets with the fewest weeks filled go first, then the **lowest current survival product**, drafting the next-best legal cell (PoolGenius win % or, on Loser, lose %). A ticket never reuses a team, skips byes, and later weeks don’t stack the same team on another ticket unless no other cell exists. Same week-1 locks are allowed; week 1 is the only week tickets may share a team by design. Local swaps then raise the joint product without moving locked weeks. Each later-week box shows the pick, **vs / @ opponent**, and weekly %. Completed weeks keep their locked projection on Season; Picks survival still counts **W = 100% / L = 0%** so a locked past pick stays on the ticket after Tuesday’s week bump instead of going blank. The score is **chance of survival**: the ticket’s product of weekly odds (finished weeks contribute 1 or 0), shown as a percent plus **1 in X** — multiplying ~11 remaining ~70% weeks naturally lands around 1%. Each row has a **Remove** (or **Clear** on the last ticket). A single **Best Path Available** button above the intro refills leftover weeks across **all tickets**, using the same joint leftover assignment (even split of the best remaining survival product). It keeps locked weeks and any pick on a prior completed week (`week < currentWeek`). Leftover fill **skips games that already have a W/L** (Thursday already final, for example) so those sides are not backfilled as 100% cells.

## NFL Survivor Loser

Route `/strategy/survivor-loser`. PoolGenius does not publish loser-pool ranks; they say the EV calculator can be used instead. This page inverts the same grid: pick the side with the **highest lose %** (`100 − PG win %`), tax teams that have an even worse future dog, and **flip the sign** of the ±4 context overlay. Used teams: `dsafd-survivor-lose-v1`. Week / Season / Picks tabs match the winner board; Picks maximizes the lose-% product. Best Path Available on Picks uses that inverted % in the same joint leftover split and still skips games that already have a W/L.

## TD Streak

Route `/strategy/td-streak`. Anytime-TD player survivor. **Week 1** is a two-primary: pick two remaining players, and **either** anytime TD advances, plus an **alternate** if a primary is inactive. For the **current week** only, players whose game is `IN_PROGRESS` or `FINAL` on `nfl-schedule-2026.csv` are skipped. The two primaries are taken from **different games**; the alt is a different remaining team/game. Weeks 2–18 are one player per week; you may set an **alternate** if the primary is inactive. Sitting as a backup does **not** use that player for later weeks. After the Tuesday job, whoever actually played on the starter card is the one tracked (green/red ring) and burned from later starter slots.

Initial board (`GET /api/analysis/td-streak`): top 80 RB/WR/TE/QB from the ESPN PPR board, plus anyone with a YTD anytime TD or ≥18 YTD PPR so a week-1 explosion is not sliced off. Ranking puts extra weight on **this year’s games**, last week’s TDs, and a **new-team bellcow** (changed NFL team + high share of that team’s positional TDs). Season path **assigns each player as a starter at most once**. It fills the hardest remaining weeks first (lowest best leftover TD%), then runs pairwise swaps that raise the **18-week survival product** (week 1 is `1 − (1−p1)(1−p2)` because either TD advances; later weeks are the starter’s TD%). Backups can be later-week starters (and the same backup can repeat); sitting as an alternate never steals a starter slot. Swap/Refresh on a **starter** moves that player here and bumps the other week. **Swap** lists **every** ESPN PPR RB/WR/TE/QB with a team who is legal that week (not on bye, and not already on another card this week, **except this week’s backup**, who can be promoted into a starter slot), grouped by position (RB, WR, TE, QB), then **% to score descending**, then positional rank — not only the top-80 path pool. Later-week starters stay in the backup list, tagged `week N starter`; picking one as an alternate leaves them as that later starter. Picking **this week’s backup** on a starter card tags them `this week's backup` and puts the outgoing starter on the alternate card.

**Matchup / TD chance** (path cards and Player board): nflverse weekly rush+rec TDs and yards (`td-player.csv`, `td-defense-by-pos.csv`, `td-player-games.csv`, `npm run enrichment:build-td-matchups`). Cards show a plain-English reason (matchup quality, how often the player scores, whether he takes most of the team’s scores at the position, last game allowed, and **home/away TDs vs this opponent** when there is a sample) plus **% to score** and Favorable / Average / Tough — not raw Off/SOS/PPR strings. TD% is mostly hit rate, positional share, opponent TDs allowed, team win %, last week, and last-game allowed. **Home/away vs this opponent** is a small extra nudge only when there are **≥2 prior games at this week’s venue vs that opponent** (capped about ±5–6 points) — the sample is usually tiny, so it does not dominate. Last-year and this-year rows use the same SOS fade: week 1 is 25% 2026 / 75% 2025, 100% this year from week 4. After a week is complete, the pick name gets a **green ring if they scored** a rush/rec TD that week and a **red ring if they did not**.

Path cards also list **rush+rec TD history** from the last **up to 5 seasons** (total TDs/games, last 5 games, then **Career stats vs** this week’s opponent: TDs/games, hit %, plus **average rush/rec/targets/yards in those vs-opp games**). Labels use the player’s NFL tenure (draft year, else first fantasy-points season): under 5 seasons → **4 years** / **Last 4 yrs**; exactly 5 → **5 years** / **Last 5 yrs**; longer than 5 → **5+ years** / **Last 5+ yrs**. The name line is **Player RB · TEAM**. Under that: **matchup (vs / at) · kickoff date (ET) · weather icon**, then **this week’s venue TD split vs that opponent** (`5 TDs in 3 home games vs CAR (Last 4 yrs)` when there is a sample on a 2023-debut player, or **No home/away game in the last N years vs CAR** when there is not), then **current OL rank** (consensus units, 1 = best) / **mismatch** (opponent D vs the position, **displayed inverted** so #1 means they cover the position well), then the favorite line (that stack uses the same **0.12rem** gap as the history stats block). The gap from the favorite line to the **% to score** pill matches the gap from the reason paragraph to those stats. The icon is a **closed-dome stadium** when `nfl-venues.json` roof is dome/retractable (or `game-context` `outdoor: false`); otherwise sun / rain / snow / wind / cold from the Open-Meteo forecast. Hover title lists temp / wind / precip and whether that is likely to change TD odds. Venue split from `td-player-games.csv` `home` (nflverse `game_id` away_home, else schedules). Click the **player name** for the same solo bio/compare dialog as search and Cheat Sheets. **Swap** and **Refresh** only change who is on that card (`slots`); they do not lock it, and both are **disabled while the card is locked**. Week 1 cards do not repeat “either TD advances.” Alternate cards do not print the inactive/backup reminder. **Lock** on a starter pins that occupant (`locked` slot keys) so later weeks will not auto-pick them as a starter and marks them used. **Lock** on a backup only pins the card — it does not burn them. **Unlocked** releases the slot so the path can refill. Open / Used on the Player board still marks the name used. After a week is final (Tuesday files), the starter-card occupant is burned automatically.

On load, the season path **re-optimizes remaining weeks around locked and finished starters**, then overlays any swapped-but-unlocked occupants on those cards. A swapped starter still occupies that week uniquely; a swapped or locked alternate can still be a later starter. Finished weeks keep their selected starter-card players (green/red rings) and burn those names. Unlocked leftover weeks get the highest leftover 18-week survival product.

A **Best Path Available** button (same pill size as the Season path / Player board tabs) sits above the two survival chips. It keeps locked cards and finished weeks, then clears unlocked leftover swaps so remaining starter (and backup) slots refill to the highest leftover 18-week survival product. Above Week 1, a chip shows **survival of the current starters**. Finished weeks count as made (100%) or missed (0%); week 1 is still either-scores. Remaining weeks use each starter’s TD%. Anytime TDs are independent weekly events, so the 18-week product is a small number even on a good path — the chip also shows **1 in X**. Directly under it, a second chip is the same product for the **next 5 weeks** (from the first unfinished week through that week + 4, or however many remain). It updates when you Swap/Refresh/Lock.

Each pick card has **Refresh**, **Swap**, and **Lock** as **large rectangle icon buttons under the card** (padlock, circular arrows, swap arrows; hover title is the old word), with the same **0.65rem** gap as between path cards. Hover and a locked padlock use a **2px green ring**, the same thickness as scored path-card borders. Path cards do **not** get a green hover border. A **locked** card keeps full opacity: unplayed locked cards use a **2px light grey `#E2E8F0` ring** (box-shadow, so the card does not resize); finished weeks keep the **green/red** scored ring and are not faded. **Refresh** and **Swap** are disabled on a locked card. Refresh cycles that slot to the next-best eligible player for the week (same missed-game / bye / different-team-and-game rules as the original assignment; skips locked names and the other cards that week). **Swap** opens a modal dropdown of **all** weekly-legal board players, grouped by position, plus **this week’s backup** when swapping a starter. Chosen swaps persist in `localStorage` `dsafd-td-streak-v1` (`slots`) without adding that key to `locked`. Lock / Unlock writes `locked`; starter locks also write `used`. Backups are not written to `altUsed` / burned.

API: `GET /api/analysis/survivor?mode=win|lose&week=`, `GET /api/analysis/td-streak`. UI: `StrategyHubPage`, `SurvivorPage`, `SurvivorPicksView`, `TdStreakPage`.
