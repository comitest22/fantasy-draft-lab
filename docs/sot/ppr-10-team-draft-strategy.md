# PPR 10-Team Draft Strategy

2026 expert consensus across FantasyPros, Fantasy Six Pack, FanDuel Research, and Draft Sharks 10-team guides: **Hero RB**, not rigid Zero RB. Leave rounds 1–3 with at least one running back. Workhorse RBs are scarce this year; WR-first is a floor build and an elite RB is the ceiling build.

## Round-by-round priorities

### Rounds 1-3: Foundation (Hero RB)
- Target elite WR/RB tiers. In PPR, WRs still gain extra value — don't reach for QB.
- Round 1 should be a high-floor, high-ceiling skill player (top-12 RB or top-8 WR).
- **Leave the first three rounds with at least one RB.** Pure Zero RB is weaker in 2026 because defined workhorses are thin.
- Round 2–3: best available RB/WR; avoid doubling up on the same risk profile (e.g., two injury-prone players).
- WR-WR-WR can still work as a floor path in 10-team PPR, but you are betting waivers and late RB luck for the ceiling.

### Rounds 4-5: Build starters
- Continue RB/WR depth. This is where league winners separate — finding WR2/RB2 value.
- TE premium only if a top-3 TE falls 1+ rounds below ADP.
- Do not take QB before round 6 unless elite tier (top-4) falls to you.

### Rounds 6-9: Upside and depth
- Target high-upside WR3/RB3 and breakout candidates.
- Consider streaming QB if no value earlier.
- Avoid reaching on rookie RBs without a clear path unless the ADP discount is significant.

### Rounds 10+: Lottery tickets and fill-ins
- Handcuffs for your RBs, high-upside WRs, backup TE.
- DST/K in last 2-3 rounds only.

## 10-team specifics
- Shallow league = talent is abundant. Prioritize ceiling over floor in mid-rounds.
- Waivers stay deep longer — you can fix mistakes. Early-round mistakes are harder to recover from.
- Snake draft: know your pick position and plan the turn (back-to-back picks in R2/3, R4/5, etc.).
- Wait on QB / K / D/ST. Streaming and late-round talent are deep enough that early spend is a tax.

## Key rules
1. Never reach more than 1 round above ADP in rounds 1-5.
2. Leave the draft with 4+ RB/WR starters viable by week 1, including at least one RB from rounds 1–3.
3. Track positional runs — join if value exists, fade if price inflated.
